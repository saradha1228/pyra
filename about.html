About Us

Our Story

PYRA began with a simple appeal on WhatsApp to help a patient meet treatment expenses. Friends and family came forward to contribute, showing how individual acts of generosity could make a meaningful difference.

What began as an informal effort grew into a shared commitment to serve people in need. PYRA was registered as a charitable trust on 27 September 2021. Today, we connect people who want to help with patients and families who need support.

Our Vision

  • A society where everyone can access essential healthcare with dignity, regardless of financial circumstances.

Our Mission

  • Support patients facing financial barriers to treatment.
  • Provide practical assistance and compassionate support to patients and families.
  • Promote cancer awareness and encourage timely medical attention.
  • Connect donors, volunteers and partners with opportunities to serve.
  • Use entrusted resources responsibly and report our work clearly.

Our Values

Compassion, accountability, togetherness and respect guide our work. We value the dignity and privacy of every patient and family.

Eligible donations may qualify for a deduction under Section 80G, subject to applicable tax rules.
