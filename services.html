Our Services

To support and change the lives of patients suffering with Cancer and Leukaemia by providing all required support by means of medical treatments, counseling & Moral support, shelter, food & Medicines, create awareness about advancement in managing Cancer etc who are otherwise unable to get proper treatments due to their financial and economic backwardness. To provide individualized prescribed rehabilitation to men, women and young people diagnosed with cancer; helping them heal as well as possible, and function at optimal levels through every stage of their treatment and recovery. Supporting families who have received a diagnosis of Cancer in all possible ways and provided with skills to use this cancer experience as an opportunity to live an enriched and productive life. To undertake health education and early detective activities in cancer and setup small/medium/large Hospital with all latest medical facilities especially for Cancer Treatments.
