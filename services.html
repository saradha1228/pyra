Our Services

Support That Responds to Real Needs

Serious illness can place financial and emotional pressure on an entire family. PYRA works to ease some of that burden through practical assistance, community support and awareness.

Medical assistance

Support towards essential medicines, diagnostic tests and treatment expenses for patients facing financial hardship.

Patient and family support

Practical help and compassionate encouragement for patients and caregivers during treatment.

Support for children

Thoughtful assistance for children undergoing treatment, including toys and gifts that offer moments of comfort and joy.

Transport support

Assistance with necessary travel expenses for hospital visits, investigations and treatment.

Awareness and education

Community campaigns and educational activities focused on cancer awareness, timely medical attention and support for affected families.

Requesting Assistance

Please contact PYRA to discuss your needs. Assistance is assessed individually and depends on eligibility, supporting documents and available resources.

Future Development Goals

PYRA aims to strengthen support for children and families, improve access to medical equipment and expand compassionate care. The initiatives below are the next steps we hope to work towards as PYRA grows.

Children’s Village & Family Strengthening

A supportive environment for children and families affected by serious illness, helping them stay together and rebuild stability.

Medical Equipment Bank

A shared pool of essential medical equipment that patients can access when they need it, easing the cost of home and hospital care.

Childhood Cancer & Family Support

Expanded services for children undergoing cancer treatment and the families who care for them, through every stage of the journey.

These initiatives are development goals. Implementation depends on planning, funding, partnerships and applicable approvals.
