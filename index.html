Welcome to PYRA Foundation

PYRA – PONDICHERRY YELLOW RIBBON ASSOCIATION is for helping people in need, financially to undergo medical treatment. Also, our PYRA Foundation believes in quality treatment for one and all and it aims to be a platform between like-minded people in coming together to make a positive impact in our society. It all started out as an idea when I first kept my WhatsApp status asking for a small donation for a particular treatment for a patient. And by God's Grace, with the help of my friends and family who were able to donate, we managed to give the required amount for their treatment, and that's when my professor asked me if I could open a WhatsApp group for it, and then the idea of opening a foundation came out, and I wanted to create a platform for like-minded people to come together for a good cause.

Facilities

Provision of cost for the tests, and treatments covered during the treatment of cancer.

Special Wishlist of toys for Children during their treatment time.

Coverage of transportation fee if the test or any procedure must be carried out elsewhere.

To set up a Palliative care for Eradication of painful demises during final stages of cancer, leukemia.

Our Services

To support and change the lives of patients suffering with Cancer and Leukaemia by providing all required support by means of medical treatments, counseling & Moral support, shelter, food & Medicines, create awareness about advancement in managing Cancer etc who are otherwise unable to get proper treatments due to their financial and economic backwardness.
