Together We Are Stronger

Supporting patients and strengthening families through compassionate care.

PYRA — Pondicherry Yellow Ribbon Association — is a charitable trust based in Puducherry, registered on 27 September 2021. We bring donors, volunteers and well-wishers together to support people facing financial difficulties during medical treatment, with a particular focus on cancer care.

Through patient assistance, family support and community awareness, we work to reduce the burden of illness on patients and their loved ones.

How We Help

Medical assistance

Support towards essential tests, medicines and treatment expenses, according to assessed needs and available resources.

Children and families

Practical assistance and encouragement for children undergoing treatment and their caregivers, including thoughtful gifts that offer comfort.

Transport assistance

Help with eligible travel expenses for hospital visits, investigations and treatment.

Community awareness

Educational outreach and cancer awareness activities that encourage timely medical attention and compassionate support.

Every Contribution Matters

Your support helps us respond to patients’ needs and stand beside families during difficult times.

Our Services

PYRA supports patients facing financial hardship during medical treatment, with a special focus on people affected by cancer and their families. Our support includes financial assistance towards essential tests, medicines and treatment, help with travel expenses, comfort for children during treatment, practical and emotional support for families, and community health awareness programmes. Assistance is offered based on assessed need and the funds available at the time.
